CONTACT FORM 7 - REFLECT NEUROPSYCHOLOGY
GENERAL INQUIRY / CALLBACK REQUEST FORM
========================================

This form is a GENERAL INQUIRY form only. It must never be positioned as an
intake, scheduling, insurance, or referral channel, and it must never collect
or transmit protected health information (PHI).

FORM CODE (paste into the Contact Form 7 "Form" tab):
-----------------------------------------------------

<div class="form-row">
    <label> Your First Name *
        [text* first-name placeholder "Enter your first name"]
    </label>

    <label> Your Last Name *
        [text* last-name placeholder "Enter your last name"]
    </label>
</div>

<label> Your Email Address *
    [email* your-email placeholder "Enter your email address"]
</label>

<label> Your Phone Number
    [tel phone placeholder "Enter your phone number (optional)"]
</label>

<label> Subject
    [select subject include_blank "General question" "Request a callback" "Services information" "Other"]
</label>

<label> Brief Message
    [textarea your-message 2x5 placeholder "Please keep this general. Do not include personal health or insurance information."]
</label>

<label class="privacy-checkbox">
    [acceptance privacy] I understand this form is for general inquiries only.
</label>

[submit "Request a Callback"]

NOTES ON THE FORM CODE:
- The subject options are deliberately limited. Do NOT add "Schedule an
  Appointment", "Insurance Questions", or "Referral Information".
- The Privacy Notice shown above the form is rendered by the theme
  (page-contact.php), NOT by CF7. Do not duplicate it here, and do not add
  extra HIPAA warnings under the message box.

MAIL SETTINGS (notification to the practice):
---------------------------------------------

To: [_site_admin_email]
From: Reflect Neuropsychology <wordpress@[_site_domain]>
Reply-To: [your-email]
Subject: Callback request from the website - [subject]

Message Body:
A general inquiry was submitted on the website.

Name: [first-name] [last-name]
Email: [your-email]
Phone: [phone]
Reason: [subject]

Reply by phone or email to continue the conversation.

--
Sent from the Reflect Neuropsychology website contact form.
Only the fields above are transmitted.

IMPORTANT - DO NOT ADD [your-message] TO THIS EMAIL.
The free-text message body is intentionally excluded from outbound
notifications. Adding it back requires explicit approval.

Use "Reply-To", not "From: [your-email]" - a visitor-controlled From address
fails SPF/DKIM and is a spoofing vector.

MAIL (2) - AUTO-REPLY:
----------------------

Leave Mail (2) DISABLED. An auto-reply echoes visitor-submitted content back
over unencrypted email and has no value for a callback request.

ADDITIONAL SETTINGS:
--------------------

Leave the Additional Settings tab EMPTY.

Specifically, do NOT add analytics conversion tracking (for example
on_sent_ok with an analytics conversion call). Contact form activity must not be sent to Google
Analytics, Google Ads, a CRM, marketing lists, Make/Zapier, Google Sheets, or
any other automation.

DATA HANDLING RULES:
--------------------

1. Do not install Flamingo or any other plugin that stores CF7 submissions in
   the WordPress database.
2. Do not forward submissions to third-party automations.
3. Do not display any practice mailbox address publicly on
   the site. Notifications go to the site admin address configured in
   Settings > General.
4. Any vendor that could receive PHI needs a Business Associate Agreement
   before it is connected.

SETUP:
------

1. Install and activate Contact Form 7.
2. Contact > Contact Forms, edit the form used on the Contact page.
3. Paste the FORM CODE above into the Form tab.
4. Configure Mail using the MAIL SETTINGS above; disable Mail (2).
5. Confirm Additional Settings is empty.
6. Save, and confirm the shortcode matches the one in page-contact.php.
7. Confirm Flamingo is not active.

TESTING:
--------

1. Submit the form and confirm delivery to the admin address.
2. Confirm the notification email contains NO message body.
3. Confirm no auto-reply is sent.
4. Confirm no submission row is stored (Flamingo absent).
5. Confirm the subject dropdown has only the four approved options.
6. Check responsive layout and required-field validation.
